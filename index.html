About FMB

Not one discipline. One point of view.

I have never worked neatly inside one professional box. My background moves through tourism, teaching, corporate training, public-sector training, communications, branding, photography, media, creative production, entrepreneurship and digital products.

That range is useful because real problems rarely arrive labeled correctly. A request for a logo may actually be a positioning problem. A request for publicity may actually be a trust problem. A request for more content may actually be a strategy problem. My role is to identify what is truly happening before deciding what should be built.

I look for the problem beneath the request, then build the clearest useful answer around it.
