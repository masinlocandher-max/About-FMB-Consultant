I am most useful when a problem needs both strategic judgment and practical execution.

How I work

A practical working process.

The exact process depends on the role or project. This is the general structure I use when the work requires strategy and execution.

01

Understand

Clarify context, objective, audience, constraints and the problem being solved.

02

Define

Set direction, priorities and boundaries before execution.

03

Design

Translate direction into a system, narrative, experience or execution plan.

04

Build

Develop the agreed work within the actual scope and resources available.

05

Review

Assess the work, identify weaknesses and refine what can be improved.
