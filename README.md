# Francine Marie Bautista | Professional Practice

Independent professional website for Francine Marie Bautista (FMB), centered on strategy, brand, communications and creative direction.

The site is intended to support consulting and project inquiries, collaborations, selected professional opportunities and professional review.

## Positioning rule

FMB is the subject of the website. No single hometown, community initiative, company or project should become the organizing identity of the portfolio.

Masinloc-related work may appear as evidence of FMB's thinking in digital product, culture, research, place, language and community systems, but it must remain one part of a broader professional body of work.

The primary professional framing is:

- Strategy
- Brand and positioning
- PR and strategic communications
- Creative direction
- Digital product direction

Training, photography, tourism, culture, language and public-interest work are supporting capabilities or evidence areas where relevant.

## Repository rule

This repository is the source of truth for this independent professional site. It must not silently inherit code, claims or unapproved assets from unrelated repositories.

For project visuals, use only assets explicitly approved for this portfolio. The approved Google Drive source remains **FMB About Me Consultant** unless a newer explicit instruction replaces it.

## Evidence standard

No hallucinated claims. No fake promises. No invented clients, employers, degrees, awards, metrics, partnerships, testimonials, outcomes, adoption figures, reach figures or impact figures.

Every project description must distinguish among:

- documented or completed work;
- work currently in development;
- concepts, intended direction or future goals.

Do not present an intended outcome as an achieved result. Do not say something was deployed, tested, verified, measured or completed unless that status has actually been confirmed.

The evidence standard should guide the writing without making every case study read like a legal disclaimer. Use one clear status or evidence note where needed, then focus the narrative on problem, insight, decision, system, execution and current state.

## Information architecture

The homepage should follow this hierarchy:

1. FMB positioning and value proposition
2. Why FMB / strategic approach
3. Capabilities
4. Selected work across multiple disciplines
5. Experience and education
6. Public-interest and cultural work, clearly separated from commercial practice
7. Engagement types and a real inquiry path

Masinloc Connect is one case study within Selected Work. It must not be visually or structurally treated as the totality of FMB's professional identity.

## Conversion rule

A call to action must lead somewhere useful. The current professional inquiry route is:

https://www.francinemariebautista.com/work-with-fmb/

Do not create fake contact information or placeholder booking systems.

## Search and accessibility rules

Public portfolio pages should be indexable unless there is an explicit reason to hide them.

Minimum expectations:

- no accidental `noindex,nofollow` on public pages;
- meaningful titles and descriptions;
- Open Graph metadata where possible;
- semantic navigation and page structure;
- visible keyboard focus states;
- reduced-motion support;
- readable interface text rather than decorative 8px or 9px copy;
- descriptive link text;
- no invented canonical URL until the deployment URL is confirmed.

## Current status

The homepage has been repositioned around FMB rather than Masinloc. Commercial practice and public-interest work are now separated, Masinloc Connect has equal rather than dominant portfolio weight, and the primary inquiry CTA routes to the verified Work With FMB page.

Future improvements should prioritize real approved visual evidence, additional case studies and deployment-specific SEO metadata without weakening the evidence standard.
